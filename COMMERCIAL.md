# Commercial license

impactite is free software under the [GNU AGPL 3.0](LICENSE). You can self-host it, change it and use it in your company at no cost, as long as you follow the AGPL. In short: if you run a changed impactite for others over a network, you share those changes under the AGPL too.

A commercial license is for when that does not fit, for example:

- you build impactite into a closed product, or offer it to your customers as part of a service, without sharing your changes
- your company does not allow AGPL software

It also comes with direct support from the maintainer and helps impactite stay fast and independent.

To get one, open an issue titled "commercial license" or write to the maintainer through the address on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/Z1xus).

The tracker in `tracker/` is MIT, so it can sit on any site without either license.
